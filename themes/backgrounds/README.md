# CEDAR wallpapers

The typography collection contains 12 designs, each rendered independently at
3840×2160 (16:9) and 2880×1800 (16:10). These are native-resolution PNGs, not
upscaled images. Pick the aspect ratio closest to your display.

| Theme | Treatment | Subtitle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evergreen | Sage on forest, spaced sans | rooted in faith |
| Parchment | Olive on parchment, serif | rooted in faith |
| Ember | Warm copper, retro shadow | rooted in faith |
| Glacier | Ice blue on slate, spaced sans | — |
| Orchid | Lavender on plum, outlined | — |
| Terminal | Green on charcoal, monospace cursor | — |
| Oxblood | Cream on burgundy, bold serif | rooted in faith |
| Solar | Charcoal on ochre, bold sans | — |
| Porcelain | Charcoal on off-white, spaced sans | rooted in faith |
| Tidal | Seafoam on deep teal, outlined | — |
| Midnight Gold | Gold on midnight, serif | rooted in faith |
| Dusk | Peach on violet, retro shadow | — |

All designs read `CEDAR`. The six subtitled designs place `rooted in faith`
below the wordmark. Filenames begin with `cedar-04-` through `cedar-15-`.

CEDAR discovers images directly in this directory. User-added wallpapers can
also live in `~/.local/share/cedar/wallpapers` or `~/.config/cedar/wallpapers`.
Adding files does not change the selected wallpaper. Open the wallpaper picker
to select one. Byte-identical local and bundled copies are deduplicated.
